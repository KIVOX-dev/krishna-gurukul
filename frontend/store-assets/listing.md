## Full description

Welcome to the Krishna Gurukul family app 🪈

Krishna Gurukul in Pattanam, Coimbatore teaches Abacus, Vedic Maths, academic tuition and Carnatic music. This app keeps parents and children close to every class, and makes daily practice something kids actually look forward to.

**For parents**
• See today's attendance and get a gentle alert if your child is marked absent
• Get test results the moment they are ready, with stars, a score ring and a kind word from "Gurukul"
• Track fees, due dates and download receipts
• Watch your child climb the class leaderboard (you can hide your child any time)
• Choose exactly which notifications you want, and a daily practice reminder at the time that suits your family

**For children**
• Practice with flash numbers, listening mode, visual sheets and a virtual abacus
• Works with no internet, so practice anywhere. Results sync when you're back online
• Cheerful animations, points, streaks and badges

**For our faculty**
• Mark attendance in seconds from the classroom
• Review results and leave encouraging remarks

**Safe by design**
• No ads, no tracking of children
• Optional fingerprint or face unlock
• You can delete your account any time from Settings

Accounts are created by the institute. Ask at the Krishna Gurukul office to get your parent login: +91 82706 65953.
